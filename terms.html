OP TCG Hub — Terms for eBay listings

Effective take 178, November 2026.

OP TCG Hub can show what is for sale on eBay in two places: a card's page (On eBay now) and the Watchlist (Ending soon on eBay, each watched card's own On eBay now, and a line on a watched card's row when one of its auctions ends within a day). These terms cover those and nothing else in the app. You accept them once, by tapping Show eBay listings in any of the panels; tap No thanks and the app shows no eBay listings, and its plain links to eBay still work. Take 178 added the questions the app asks on its own when it opens; a yes you gave before it stands and covers them, and you can turn eBay listings off at any time.

What you agree to

  1. You are bound by eBay's API License Agreement as it applies to the eBay listings the app shows you.
  2. eBay is a third-party beneficiary of these terms.
  3. Your permission to see eBay listings in the app can be withdrawn at any time, by eBay or by the app.

You see the listings only through the app: it gives you no way to call eBay's services yourself, and its key to them is never shared.

What the panels show

On a card's page, English listings of the card, asked for when you open the panel while online: each listing's price and shipping as eBay gives them, in eBay's currency, with a link to the listing on eBay. They are asking prices, not sales and not a value. The app sorts each listing to the card's printing where the listing's title allows, and says so where it cannot tell. Buying happens on eBay, under eBay's own terms, between you and the seller.

On the Watchlist, English auctions of the cards you watch, in one list: each auction's bid and shipping as eBay gives them, when it ends, which watched card it is for, and a link to it on eBay. A bid is a bid, not a sale and not a value. A watched card's own panel shows that card's listings as its page does. A watched card's row says when an auction the app reads as that card's ends within 24 hours.

When the app asks. A panel asks when you open it while online. Once you have accepted these terms, the app also asks about your watched cards' auctions on its own: at once when you accept, when you open the app, and again about every two hours while it stays open (sooner, from a quarter of an hour on, after a try the server refused or did not answer), online only, and on mobile data only if you have allowed quiet updates there. That is what lets a row show an auction's end without a panel being open.

What leaves the phone

To ask, the app sends the card's number, and a grade when you ask for a graded copy's listings, to this project's own server, which asks eBay and keeps the answer for a short while so the same question is not asked twice. On the Watchlist, and when it asks on its own, it sends the number of each card you watch, and its grade when you watch it at one, one card at a time. Nothing from your collection is sent. The privacy policy says more.

Turning it off

Tap Turn eBay listings off under any of the panels: the app then asks nothing about eBay, on its own or otherwise. You can turn them on again from the same place.
