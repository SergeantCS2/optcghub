## Short description (80)
Scan, value and track your One Piece Card Game collection. Offline. Unlimited scans.

## Full description (4000)

Not affiliated with Bandai, Shueisha, Toei Animation, Viz Media or TCGplayer.
Card names and prices are theirs; this app is a tool for your collection.

**Scan without a cap.** Point the camera at a card and OP TCG Hub reads the
printed number, then shows you exactly which printings share it — the base,
the alternate art, the SP — because the same number can be worth $0.07 or
$467. Scanning is free and unlimited, always.

**Know what it's worth.** Every card carries the TCGplayer market price and the
day it was fetched, with the low-to-high spread shown rather than hidden. Your
collection totals up, moves day over day, and Market Movers shows what shifted
overnight. Condition is what you tell it; nothing is invented.

**Build legal decks.** Pick a Leader, add fifty cards, and the builder checks
the official Comprehensive Rules as you go — colour, the four-copy limit, the
count — and shows the cost curve, counters, blockers and triggers. Import or
export lists as "4 OP01-016 Nami".

**Trade with numbers.** Your side from your collection, theirs pasted as a
list, both valued the same way, and the difference stated plainly.

**Yours, offline.** The catalogue is on your phone. Scanning, valuing, building
and saving all work with no signal. A backup is written to Documents on every
save and survives uninstalling. Export to CSV any time — no account, no lock-in,
and nothing you do is sent anywhere.

Supported by short rewarded ads that unlock new saves. Scanning itself is never
gated, and nothing already in your collection ever is.

Catalogue and prices from TCGplayer via TCGCSV.
