OP TCG Hub — Privacy

Effective take 23, September 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing, itself. There is no account. Your collection, decks, portfolios and photos live on your phone and in a backup file in your phone's Documents folder. The app does not send them anywhere and has no server to send them to.

The camera

The camera is used to read the printed number on a card. The photo it takes stays on the device as that card's picture in your collection. Nothing from the camera leaves the phone.

Ads

The app shows short rewarded ads from Google AdMob, which you choose to watch to unlock saves. To serve them, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID and related device identifiers and shares them with Google and its advertising partners. This is the only data that leaves the phone, and it goes to Google, not to us. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings at any time.

Prices and card images

Prices and the card catalogue come from TCGplayer via TCGCSV, downloaded as one file when you sync. Reference card images are fetched from TCGplayer's image server when shown and are not stored. These requests carry no identifier from the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
