    more than four of any card number. Ten DON!! cards ride alongside and are not
    built. Comprehensive Rules v1.2.0 §5-1 — the full text is in the app’s
    documentation and on Bandai’s site.

A turn, from the rules

    
Refresh — DON!! return, everything untaps. Draw one (not on the first player’s first turn).
    DON!! — two from the DON!! deck (one on the first player’s first turn). Main — play, give DON!! for +1000 each,
    attack. End. §6-1. Attack the Leader or a rested Character; attacker’s power ≥ defender’s wins (§7-1-4).
